title: WM199 · Developing an Engineering Mindset
---
**Block 1 · 15 credits.** The professional side of engineering: what being a registered engineer means, how to keep developing, and how to make decisions that are ethical, sustainable and safe.

**Assessment:** A1 professional development portfolio, 30% (week 5) · A2 functional requirements report on a case study, 70% (week 9).

The running example in the seminars is a **campus delivery robot** that moves equipment between labs. It's a good test case for every topic below.

## Week 1: the professional engineer
1. [[UK-SPEC and professional registration]]: the five competence areas and EngTech, IEng, CEng
2. [[CPD and reflective practice]]: Gibbs' cycle, SMART goals and evidencing growth
3. [[Writing a problem statement]]: interrogating a brief before solving it

## Week 2: responsibility
1. [[Engineering ethics and duty of care]]: ethical theories, the five principles, real dilemmas
2. [[Sustainability and lifecycle thinking]]: the triple bottom line, LCA and the circular economy

## Week 3: hazards, risk and safe design
1. [[Hazards and risk assessment]]: hazard → harm, risk matrices, registers and controls
2. [[Designing for safety]]: inherent safety, safe failure, Swiss cheese, HAZOP, FMEA and safe systems of work
