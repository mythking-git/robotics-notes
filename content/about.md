I'm Mikey, a first-year student on Warwick's **Robotics Engineering with AI** BEng (WMG). I write these guides to understand the material properly, and in the hope they help someone else.

## How they're made
- Each guide is built from my own lecture notes, then rewritten to explain the topic from first principles.
- **AI was used to help draft and structure the guides, generate diagrams and check calculations.** I've checked the content, but mistakes are possible. If you spot one, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/mythking-git/robotics-notes).
- The worked examples use my own numbers. Datasheet values (e.g. the IRFP140 and IRFP250 MOSFETs) are quoted as facts, with the part named so you can check the source.

## What's not here
- No lecture slides, Moodle files or assessment answers.
- No assessed work. Anything I've submitted for marks stays private.

If you're on the same course: these are a companion, not a replacement. Go to the sessions and do the problems yourself.
