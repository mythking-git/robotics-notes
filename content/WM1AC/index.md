title: WM1AC · Mechanisms and Machines

**Block 1 · 15 credits.** How things move and why: the kinematics and kinetics of particles, rigid bodies and mechanisms like gear trains and slider-cranks. The maths is taught alongside the mechanics, so each week has a maths topic and an engineering topic that uses it.

**Assessment:** A1 computer-based test, 30% (week 5, formula sheet provided) · A2 individual lab report on a geared slider-crank, 70% (week 9).

**Textbook:** Hibbeler, *Engineering Mechanics: Dynamics* (and *Statics*), 14th ed.

## Week 1: calculus, vectors and straight-line motion
1. [[Calculus for kinematics]]: differentiation and integration, the way mechanics uses them
2. [[Vectors]]: components, dot product and cross product
3. [[Matrices and Cramer's rule]] (shared with WM1AD)
4. [[Rectilinear motion]]: position, velocity and acceleration in a straight line

## Week 2: curves, rotation and implicit differentiation
1. [[Curvilinear and circular motion]]: motion along a curve, tangential and normal acceleration
2. [[Rigid body rotation]]: translation, fixed-axis rotation and general plane motion
3. [[Implicit differentiation]]: differentiating geometry with respect to time

## Week 3: mechanisms
1. [[Gear trains and belt drives]]: what's shared at a contact
2. [[Absolute motion analysis]]: geometry → velocity → acceleration
3. [[Relative motion analysis]]: $\mathbf v_B = \mathbf v_A + \boldsymbol\omega \times \mathbf r_{B/A}$

## Coming next
Week 4: forces, moments, static equilibrium, Newton's second law and centre of mass.
