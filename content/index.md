Study guides for **Robotics Engineering with AI (BEng), Year 1, WMG, University of Warwick**, written as I go through the course.

These aren't lecture transcripts. Each guide takes one topic and explains it from scratch: the idea in plain English, a diagram, the formulas derived step by step, a worked example with fresh numbers, the mistakes people make, and a few questions to check yourself.

## How to use these
- Read the **idea** first, then try the **worked example** yourself before opening the answer.
- The **Check yourself** questions at the end of each guide have hidden answers. Click to reveal.
- Every symbol is defined where it's first used. The module page has a full formula sheet.
