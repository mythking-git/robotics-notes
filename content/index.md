Study guides for **Robotics Engineering with AI (BEng), Year 1, WMG, University of Warwick**, written as I work through the course.

These aren't lecture transcripts. Each guide takes **one topic** and explains it from scratch: the idea in plain English, a diagram, the formulas derived step by step, a worked example with fresh numbers, the usual mistakes, and a few questions to check yourself.

## How to use these guides
1. Read **the idea** first. If that makes sense, the maths will too.
2. Cover the answer and try the **worked example** yourself.
3. Do the **Check yourself** questions. The answers are hidden until you click.
4. Use each module's **formula sheet** for revision.
