A blank log to copy into your own notes. Record things **when they happen**: by the time you apply for a placement or professional registration, you won't remember the details. See [[CPD and reflective practice]] for how to reflect well.

## Reflection prompt (Gibbs, short version)
For anything significant, add a few lines:
- **What happened?**
- **How did it go, and why?**
- **What did I learn?**
- **What will I do differently next time?**

## Review
Every few weeks: which goals moved forward, what's stalled, and what's the next goal?
