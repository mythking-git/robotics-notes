Copy this into your own notes and fill it in at the start of each term. Compare it with the last one to see how you've moved. The five areas are explained in [[UK-SPEC and professional registration]].

**Scale:** 1 = no experience yet · 2 = aware, little practice · 3 = done it with support · 4 = can do it independently · 5 = could teach or lead it

## Tips
- **Score from evidence, not feeling.** If you can't name something you did, it's probably a 1 or 2.
- Pick **one strength and one or two gaps** to focus on, and turn each gap into a SMART goal in the [[CPD log template]].
- Keep old versions. Seeing a 2 become a 4 over a year is useful evidence in itself.
