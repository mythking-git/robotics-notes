## Trackr
[Trackr](https://the-trackr.com) is a jobs platform for students that lists **spring weeks, internships and graduate roles**, with separate trackers by sector, including **UK engineering** and **UK tech**. Each tracker lists open roles in one place so you can see what's open and plan around deadlines. It also has a CV template, CV reviews, guides and community group chats. There's a free account and a paid Premium tier.

**How to use it well:**
1. Make an account and open the **UK Engineering** and **UK Tech** trackers. Robotics roles turn up in both.
2. Go through it once a week, early in the season (September–December), and note opening dates.
3. Keep your own tracker of what you've applied for (company, role, date applied, stage, deadline, notes). A spreadsheet or an Obsidian table is fine.
4. Apply early. Rolling deadlines mean the advertised closing date is the latest, not the target.

## Other places to look
| Where | Good for |
|---|---|
| **Gradcracker** | Engineering and technology placements, internships and graduate jobs in the UK |
| **The university careers service** | Local opportunities, CV and interview help, careers fairs, employer events |
| **Professional institutions** (IET, IMechE) | Student membership, events, scholarships, and employers who sponsor students |
| **LinkedIn** | Following companies, and seeing who's been on which schemes |
| **Company careers pages** | The final, official deadlines. Always check here |
| **Lecturers and lab staff** | Summer research projects and industry contacts |
| **Societies and competitions** | Robotics clubs and hackathons, where employers often sponsor and recruit |

## Scholarships worth knowing about
Some institutions and employers fund engineering students with money, mentoring and work experience. The IET and IMechE both run scholarship schemes. Check their sites each autumn.

## Check yourself
> [!question]- 1. A role says "closes 31 January" but is rolling. When should you apply?
> As soon as it opens. Rolling roles can fill and close long before the stated date.
